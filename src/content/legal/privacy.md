This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit **jpxoi.com**, send an enquiry or book a meeting. The person responsible for this website is **Jean Paul Fernandez**. Only the information needed to operate the site and respond to people who make contact is collected. Personal information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

## Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is **Jean Paul Fernandez**. jpxoi.com is his personal website and professional brand, operated from Peru and the United Kingdom. Privacy enquiries should be sent to [privacy@jpxoi.com](mailto:privacy@jpxoi.com).

## Scope of this policy

This policy applies only to jpxoi.com. The website contains links to project websites, social networks and other third-party services. Those services operate independently and their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

## Information collected and why

### Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are collected. This information is used to read, manage and respond to your enquiry, and to take steps at your request before entering into a potential contract. An additional lawful basis is the legitimate interest in communicating with prospective clients, collaborators and employers and maintaining appropriate business records.

The form is processed by Formcarry. Formcarry may also process limited submission metadata, such as your IP address, browser, operating system, time zone and referring page, to deliver the submission, maintain security and prevent spam. Notifications are delivered to email accounts provided by Apple and Google.

### Meeting bookings

If you choose to book a meeting, Cal.com processes your name, email address, selected date and time, time zone, any additional notes and details of guests you add. This information is used to arrange and manage the meeting at your request. Calendar invitations and the Google Meet link are handled through Google Calendar and Google Meet.

Cal.com is embedded in this website, but its booking interface and processing are also subject to the [Cal.com Privacy Policy](https://cal.com/privacy).

Providing contact or booking information is optional and is not a statutory requirement. A response or meeting cannot be arranged without the required name, email and message or booking details.

### Website delivery and security

This website is hosted by Vercel, while images and other static assets are delivered from Cloudflare R2. These providers may process routine technical request information, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested URLs, timestamps and security logs, to deliver the website reliably, diagnose errors and protect it from abuse. This processing is based on the legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website.

### Privacy-focused analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used to understand aggregate page views and website performance. According to Cloudflare, this service does not collect or use visitors' personal data and does not use cookies or localStorage for analytics. These aggregate insights are used only to improve the website; they are not used for advertising or to build individual visitor profiles.

### Theme preference

If you change the colour theme, your browser stores that preference locally under `selected-theme`. It remains on your device and is used only to remember your choice. You can remove it by clearing this website's local storage in your browser.

## Service providers

The following providers are used only where needed to operate the website and respond to enquiries:

- **Vercel** — website hosting, delivery, reliability and security.
- **Cloudflare** — static asset delivery through R2 and privacy-focused web analytics.
- **Formcarry** — contact form delivery, storage and spam protection.
- **Apple and Google** — email delivery and correspondence.
- **Cal.com** — scheduling and management of meeting bookings.
- **Google** — calendar invitations and Google Meet conferencing.

These providers process information under their own contractual, security and privacy commitments. They are not authorised to use contact or booking information for their own advertising.

## International transfers

Because this website is operated from Peru and the United Kingdom and uses international service providers, information may be processed in Peru, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, the United States or other locations in which those providers operate. Formcarry states that form data is stored in the European Economic Area. Cal.com states that it processes data in the United States and uses recognised transfer mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or applicable adequacy frameworks, where required.

Where data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the relevant provider's contractual protections, adequacy mechanism or other lawful transfer safeguard is relied on.

## How long information is kept

- Contact enquiries, related email correspondence and booking details are normally kept for up to 12 months from the last meaningful interaction.
- If an enquiry develops into a professional or contractual relationship, relevant records may be retained for the duration of that relationship and afterwards for as long as reasonably needed for legal, tax, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.
- Technical security and hosting logs are retained according to the operational schedules of Vercel and Cloudflare. Aggregate analytics may be retained for longer because they are not used to identify individual visitors.
- Your theme preference remains on your device until you change it or clear your browser storage.

Service-provider backups may retain limited copies for a short additional period before they are overwritten or deleted through their standard retention cycle.

## When information may be disclosed

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid request from a competent authority, establish or defend legal claims, or protect the security and rights of the website, its visitors or others. Personal information is not sold or rented.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions, and identity verification may be required before a request is completed.

To exercise a privacy right, email [privacy@jpxoi.com](mailto:privacy@jpxoi.com). A response will be provided within the period required by the applicable law.

You may also lodge a complaint with the authority that applies to your circumstances, including the [Peruvian National Authority for Personal Data Protection (ANPD)](https://www.gob.pe/anpd) or the [UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Cookies and similar technologies

jpxoi.com does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics operates without cookies or client-side storage. The local theme preference described above is functional browser storage, not an advertising or tracking mechanism. If you open the Cal.com scheduler, Cal.com's necessary technologies and privacy terms apply to that interaction.

## Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures and reputable service providers are used to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not include passwords, payment details, health information or other sensitive information in the contact form or meeting notes.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the website, contact [privacy@jpxoi.com](mailto:privacy@jpxoi.com) so that it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.
